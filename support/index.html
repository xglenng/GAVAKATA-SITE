Customer support

Get help with Maia Front Desk, your software subscription, or a Gavakata Software project.

Contact support

Email garrett@gavakatasoftware.com with a short description of what you need help with.

Email support ↗

Help us find the issue

  • Your studio or business name and the email associated with your account.
  • The page or feature involved, what you expected, and what happened.
  • The approximate time and any error message. If you attach a screenshot, hide client details and payment information.

Never email passwords, authentication codes, API keys, full card numbers, identity documents, or private client records.

Subscriptions and billing

For subscription questions, cancellation requests, or a charge you do not recognize, email support with your business name, charge date, and amount. We will review the issue with you. Do not send your card details.

Studio appointments and purchases

For an appointment, deposit, refund, service, or merchandise purchase from a studio using Maia, contact that studio first. The studio sets its prices and booking, cancellation, and refund policies. Gavakata provides the software.

Privacy and account access

Contact garrett@gavakatasoftware.com for privacy requests, account access problems, or suspected unauthorized activity. For information a studio holds about you, contact the studio so it can verify and handle your request.
